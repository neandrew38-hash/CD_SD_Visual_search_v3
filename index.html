Help
How HaulHawk works, screen by screen
Your searches
Searches is the first tab. At the top: how many searches are on, paused or off, and how many alerts you got today. 🌙 / ☀️ switches dark and light theme, ⏸ pauses all your searches at once (tap again to resume them). The chips below filter the list by platform.
Each card shows the search name, then where from → where to and the filters as small chips. The switch turns the search on or off. At the bottom: alerts today and last … ago — when its last alert came. Buttons: ⏸ Pause, 📑 Duplicate, ✏️ Edit, 🗑 Delete. Tap the card itself to edit it.
⏸ Pause asks for how long. The search keeps its place and turns itself back on when the time is up. Tap ▶ to resume early.
New search (the orange button at the bottom) opens an empty search.
Building a search
1 · Platforms — tick where to search: Central Dispatch, Super Dispatch, Ship.Cars, Autosled. One search can run on several at once.
2 · Route — Origin and Destination take cities and whole states together. Type a city or ZIP and pick it from the list; its radius (mi) is the number on its chip (the box next to the field sets the radius for the next city). Typing a state name, e.g. nevada, offers the whole state first. × removes a chip, ⇅ swaps Origin and Destination.
States opens the state picker: tap states one by one, or a region (West, Southwest, Midwest, Southeast, Northeast) to add it all at once — tap the region again to remove it. Search state narrows the grid, Done closes it.
Search along route — needs exactly one city in Origin and one in Destination (not a state). Finds loads whose route passes near that path, within Distance off route. Optionally add up to 5 Waypoints — cities the route goes through, in order. Works on all platforms.
3 · Vehicles — vehicle types (pick one or more, or none for all types; for Ship.Cars the bot works the type out from the model, e.g. Honda Odyssey → Mini-van). Number of vehicles: Min and Max with − / + (Max Any = no limit). Condition and Trailer: Both, or just one kind.
4 · Price & dates — only loads paying at least this total or this $ per mile. Ready to ship within: slide to only get loads ready in that many days.
5 · Blocked companies — type part of a company name and tap +. Loads from them are skipped in this search only. To block a company everywhere use More → Blocked companies (or the 🚫 Block button under an alert).
Save / Save & start — the line above the buttons sums up the search before you save. Save & start saves it and turns it on right away.
Alerts in the chat
Every new matching load is sent to you as soon as it appears. All sites (Central Dispatch, Super Dispatch, Ship.Cars, Autosled) look the same:

Load ID — tap it to open exactly this load on the site.
🔁 reposted — the shipper posted this same load again (usually to push it back to the top). You get it again so you don't miss it. (was $1,050) means the price changed - it used to be $1,050.
Vehicles — every car on the load, numbered, with its type. Tap a car to Google its size and curb weight.
Route info — Origin and Destination; tap a city to open it on Google Maps.
💳 — total pay | miles | $ per mile. The line under it is how you get paid: COD = cash/certified from the customer on delivery; Company check / ACH / Comchek… = the shipper pays you later (with the terms, e.g. "30 business days after signed BOL"); "method not specified" = the shipper pays later but didn't say how.
💰 +14% vs this lane — this load pays 14% more per mile than similar loads (same states, similar distance) paid over the last 7 days; "avg" is that usual rate. 🔻 −20% means it pays less than usual. It only shows up when the difference is big (10%+ above or 15%+ below) and the bot has seen enough loads on that lane to be sure.
Pickup date, Trailer type (Enclosed 🚚 when enclosed) and Vehicle status (Operable ✅ / Inoperable ⛔️). Additional info📝 = the shipper's own notes, when there are any.
🏢 Company — tap to open their profile; below it their rating and phone number (tap the number to copy it).
The last line shows which of your searches found it and on which site.
Buttons: 📞 Call — call the company (see below) · 📋 SMS — copies your ready message (edit it in the ✉️ SMS tab) · 🗺 Route — driving directions · ⭐ Save — keep the load for later · 🚫 Block — no more alerts from that company.
Call button
Tap 📞 Call under an alert: a small page opens and then your calling app opens with the number already typed in. It does not start the call by itself — you press call when you're ready.
It opens Quo by default. Using a different app or your normal phone? Send /call to the bot and pick Phone (regular call) — then it opens your phone's regular dialer (or whatever calling app you set as default). You can switch back any time.
On a phone — you need the Quo app installed and logged in.
On a computer — it opens your computer's default calling app. To make that Quo:
Windows: Settings → Apps → Default apps, search TEL, choose Quo.
Mac: FaceTime → Settings → Default for calls → Quo.
If nothing opens, tap the big button on that page, or use 📋 Copy number there.
Saved & More
⭐ Saved (second tab at the bottom) — loads you saved with ⭐ Save under an alert (newest first, up to 50), each with 📞 Call, 📋 Copy number and 🗑 Remove. /favorites in the chat works too.
🚫 Blocked companies — no alerts from these companies on any site or search. Block one with 🚫 Block under an alert or type its name here; Unblock undoes it.
✉️ SMS template — the message 📋 SMS copies. Tags {{vehicle}} {{origin}} {{dest}} {{price}} {{company}} {{loadId}} are filled in from each load. It is only yours.
