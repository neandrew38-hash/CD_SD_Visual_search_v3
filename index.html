❓ Help

🔎 Building a search

Origin / Destination — type a city and set its radius (mi), or tap a state on the grid for the whole state. Use ⇅ to swap them.
Min Total Pay / Min $/mile — only show loads paying at least this total, or at least this rate per mile.
Vehicle Status, Trailer Type and Min / Max # of Vehicles — tap to filter and to pick how many vehicles per load.
Ready to ship — slide to only show loads ready within that many days.
Vehicle Type — Car, SUV, Pickup, Van, Mini-van, Motorcycle, ATV… pick one or more; leave empty for all types. Ship.Cars emails don't say the type, so for Ship.Cars the bot works it out from the model name (e.g. Honda Odyssey → Mini-van, Ford F-150 → Pickup).
Search along route — needs exactly one Origin city and one Destination city. Finds loads whose route passes near that path, within the distance (mi) you set. Optionally add up to 5 Waypoints (cities the route should pass through, in order) — works on all platforms.
🚫 Exclude companies — type part of a broker/company name and tap ➕ (there's no list to pick from). Loads from them are never sent to you.
💾 Save / ▶ Save & start (the green button) — saves your search and starts it right away; it keeps running and alerts you about new loads. Every search has it, new or edited.

🚗 Ship.Cars

Tick "Ship.Cars" alongside Central Dispatch/Super Dispatch (or on its own) — Origin/Destination, price, $/mile, Vehicle Type and Exclude companies apply to it too.
How it works: when you save the search, the bot creates a saved lane for it on Ship.Cars by itself. Ship.Cars then emails each new load on that lane, the bot checks it against your filters and sends it to you — usually within a minute or two.
"Search along route" works for Ship.Cars too (start and end city; waypoints are checked by the bot, Ship.Cars itself only knows start and end).
Company name and phone number aren't always shown for Ship.Cars alerts — that's a Ship.Cars limitation, not a bug. No phone = no 📞 Call button on that alert.

🛷 Autosled

Tick "Autosled" next to the other sites — Origin/Destination (states, cities with radius, Search along route), price, $/mile, trailer, operable/inoperable, number of vehicles and Vehicle Type all work.
Autosled itself is the shipper on every load, so alerts have no company or phone (no 📞 Call / 🚫 Block buttons). Tap the Load ID or 🏢 Autosled to open the load and book it on the site.
Vehicle Type on Autosled is by class: Car, SUV, Pickup (Autosled calls it Truck), Van. Autosled doesn't separate mini-vans from vans, and usually doesn't give the make/model — the alert shows the class, weight and size instead.

📋 Managing your searches

Each search has, left to right: ⏸/▶ Pause, 📑 Duplicate, an on/off switch, ✏️ Edit and 🗑 Delete. 📨 N next to it shows how many loads it has sent you (total, and today).
Tapping ⏸ lets you pick how long to pause - it keeps your place and picks back up on its own, no need to remember to turn it back on. Tap ▶ to resume early.

Load ID — tap it to open exactly this load on the site.
🔁 reposted — the shipper posted this same load again (usually to push it back to the top). You get it again so you don't miss it. (was $850) means the price changed - it used to be $850.
Vehicles — every car on the load, numbered, with its type. Tap a car to Google its size and curb weight.
Route info — Origin and Destination; tap a city to open it on Google Maps.
💳 — total pay | miles | $ per mile. The line under it is how you get paid: COD = cash/certified from the customer on delivery; Company check / ACH / Comchek… = the shipper pays you later (with the terms, e.g. "30 business days after signed BOL"); "method not specified" = the shipper pays later but didn't say how.
💰 +18% vs this lane — this load pays 18% more per mile than similar loads (same states, similar distance) paid over the last 7 days; "avg" is that usual rate. 🔻 −20% means it pays less than usual. It only shows up when the difference is big (10%+ above or 15%+ below) and the bot has seen enough loads on that lane to be sure.
Pickup date, Trailer type (Enclosed 🚚 when enclosed) and Vehicle status (Operable ✅ / Inoperable ⛔️). Additional info📝 = the shipper's own notes, when there are any.
🏢 Company — tap to open their profile; below it their rating and phone number (tap the number to copy it).
The last line shows which of your searches found it and on which site.
Buttons: 📞 Call — call the company (see below) · 📋 SMS — copies your ready message (edit it in the ✉️ SMS tab) · 🗺 Route — driving directions · ⭐ Save — keep the load for later · 🚫 Block — no more alerts from that company.

📞 Call button

Tap 📞 Call under an alert: a small page opens and then your calling app opens with the number already typed in. It does not start the call by itself — you press call when you're ready.
It opens Quo by default. Using a different app or your normal phone? Send /call to the bot and pick Phone (regular call) — then it opens your phone's regular dialer (or whatever calling app you set as default). You can switch back any time.
On a phone — you need the Quo app installed and logged in.
On a computer — it opens your computer's default calling app. To make that Quo:
Windows: Settings → Apps → Default apps, search TEL, choose Quo.
Mac: FaceTime → Settings → Default for calls → Quo.
If nothing opens, tap the big button on that page, or use 📋 Copy number there.

⭐ Saved loads

Tap ⭐ Save under any alert — the button turns into a green ✅ Saved so you know it worked.
All your saved loads are in the ⭐ Saved tab at the bottom of this app (newest first, up to 50). Each one has 📞 Call, 📋 Copy number and 🗑 Remove. Tap a load's ID, city or company to open it.
The old big "⭐ My saved loads" button under the chat is gone to free up space. Typing /favorites in the chat still works too.

🚫 Blocked companies

Tap 🚫 Block under an alert to stop all loads from that company - on every site and every one of your searches. The 🚫 Blocked tab lists them: tap Unblock to undo, or type a company name there to block it before it ever shows up.
Different from Exclude companies in a search: Exclude works only for that one search, Block works for all of them.

✉️ SMS template

The ✉️ SMS tab holds the message that 📋 SMS copies. Use the tags {vehicle} {origin} {dest} {price} {company} {loadId} — they're filled in from each load. Only you use it — changing it doesn't affect anyone else.
