❓ Help

🔎 Building a search

Origin / Destination — type a city and set its radius (mi), or tap a state on the grid for the whole state. Use ⇅ to swap them.
Min Total Pay / Min $/mile — only show loads paying at least this total, or at least this rate per mile.
Vehicle Status, Trailer Type and Min / Max # of Vehicles — tap to filter and to pick how many vehicles per load.
Ready to ship — slide to only show loads ready within that many days.
Vehicle Type — filter by ATV, Car, SUV, etc.
Search along route — needs exactly one Origin city and one Destination city. Finds loads whose route passes near that path, within the distance (mi) you set.
🚫 Exclude companies — comma-separated company names; loads from them are never sent to you.
Save — starts the search right away; it keeps running and alerts you about new loads.

📋 Managing your searches

⏸ Pause — stop a search for a few hours without deleting it; it resumes on its own, no need to remember.
📑 Duplicate — copies a search's filters into a new one, so you can tweak just one thing instead of starting over.
📨 N sent — shows how many loads a search has alerted you about, total and today.

🔔 Alerts

Every new matching load is sent to you as soon as it appears.
Tap the vehicle name — searches Google for its specs (dimensions, curb weight).
Tap the 🆔 Order ID or 📍 route — opens the search results page where that load is listed.
Tap the 🏢 company name — opens their profile page.
🗺 Route button — opens driving directions on Google Maps.
📋 SMS template button — copies your ready-to-send message for that load.
